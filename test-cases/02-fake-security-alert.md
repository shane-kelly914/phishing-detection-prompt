# Test Case 02: Fake Security Alert

**Expected Risk Level:** High
**Scenario:** Credential-harvesting attempt impersonating a generic "Security Team"

## Email

> Subject: Action Required: Unusual Login Attempt Detected
>
> Dear Customer,
>
> We detected a login attempt from an unrecognized device. For your security, your account has been temporarily limited.
>
> Please verify your identity immediately to restore full access: http://secure-account-verify-login.com
>
> Failure to verify within 24 hours may result in permanent suspension.
>
> Sincerely, Security Team

## Why This Test Matters

Tests detection of classic phishing indicators: urgency, generic greeting, suspicious unbranded domain, vague sender, and fear-based manipulation.
